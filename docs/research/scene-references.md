# Scene references

The 45 references on the Rain & Lamplight moodboard (2026-11-09), by group. Images came from Steam store
screenshots and Wikimedia Commons; the raw list is `data/moodboard-refs.json`. Credits and years are from memory and
were not double-checked.

## Pixel & lo-fi

Low resolution, limited palettes, rooms as little worlds. Close to the first prototype.

| Reference | By | Year | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Coffee Talk](https://store.steampowered.com/app/914800/) | Toge Productions | 2020 | A late-night café in a rainy city. The street is just the window behind the counter. |
| [A Space for the Unbound](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1201270/) | Mojiken | 2023 | Dense, lush pixel art of small-town Indonesia. Lamps, clutter and night air. |
| [Kind Words](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1070710/) | Popcannibal | 2019 | One tiny isometric bedroom floating in the dark. Lo-fi as a place you sit in. |
| [Kind Words 2 (lofi city pop)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2118120/) | Popcannibal | 2025 | The same idea at night: a warm room you look into, with the city outside. |
| [Spirit City: Lofi Sessions](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2113850/) | Mooncat Games | 2023 | Literally a room to sit in while music plays, built around a big window. |
| [Unpacking](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1135690/) | Witch Beam (Brisbane) | 2021 | Rooms that tell a story through their objects alone. Made in Brisbane. |

## Paintings & woodblock prints

Where most of this comes from: lamplight, wet streets, quiet rooms.

| Reference | By | Year | Why it's here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Nighthawks](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nighthawks_by_Edward_Hopper_1942.jpg) | Edward Hopper | 1942 | The classic inside-versus-outside picture: a lit diner on a dark street. |
| [Boar Lane, Leeds, by Lamplight](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Atkinson_Grimshaw_Boar_Lane_Leeds_by_lamplight_1881.jpg) | John Atkinson Grimshaw | 1881 | Wet streets glowing gold from shop windows. Grimshaw painted this feeling over and over. |
| [Liverpool Quay by Moonlight](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Atkinson_Grimshaw_%281836-1893%29_-_Liverpool_Quay_by_Moonlight_-_T00902_-_Tate.jpg) | John Atkinson Grimshaw | 1887 | Moonlight on a wet road with every shop window lit. |
| [Paris Street; Rainy Day](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gustave_Caillebotte_-_Paris_Street%3B_Rainy_Day_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg) | Gustave Caillebotte | 1877 | Calm, grey, everyone under umbrellas. Rain as a mood rather than a storm. |
| [Rainy Day, Columbus Avenue, Boston](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hassam_-_columbus-avenue-rainy-day-1885.jpg) | Childe Hassam | 1885 | Soft brown-grey air and reflections on the road. |
| [Café Terrace at Night](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vincent_Willem_van_Gogh_-_Cafe_Terrace_at_Night_%28Yorck%29.jpg) | Vincent van Gogh | 1888 | A yellow café against a blue night: the warm/cool split in its purest form. |
| [Interior with Woman Seen from the Back](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vilhelm_Hammershoi_-_Interieur_mit_Rueckenansicht_einer_Frau_-_1903-1904_-_Randers_Kunstmuseum.jpg) | Vilhelm Hammershøi | 1903–04 | Total stillness: a quiet room and soft light. Peace without any rain at all. |
| [Sudden Shower over Shin-Ōhashi Bridge](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hiroshige%2C_Sudden_shower_over_Shin-%C5%8Chashi_bridge_and_Atake%2C_1857.jpg) | Utagawa Hiroshige | 1857 | Rain drawn as fine lines over flat colour. The original paper-cut rain. |
| [Night at Shinkawa](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:T%C5%8Dky%C5%8D_j%C5%ABnidai%2C_Yoru_no_Shinkawa_by_Kawase_Hasui.jpg) | Kawase Hasui | 1919 | One lamp on a dark wall at night, printed in flat layers. |
| [Evening at Ushibori](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kawase_Hasui_%281930s%29_Evening_of_Ushibori.jpg) | Kawase Hasui | 1930 | Still water and dusk blues. Calm made out of a few flat colours. |
| [Kagurazaka Street after a Night Rain](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kagurazaka_Street_at_Night_after_Rain%2C_from_the_Series_%22Twelve_Scenes_of_Tokyo%22._Hiroshi_Yoshida.jpg) | Hiroshi Yoshida | 1929 | Shop fronts glowing after rain, printed in layers. Very close to the café brief. |
