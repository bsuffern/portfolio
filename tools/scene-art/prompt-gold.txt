Shin-hanga Japanese woodblock print in the style of Kawase Hasui and Hiroshi Yoshida. View from inside a small dim wooden café at night, looking out through a large three-pane window with a lattice strip at the top. On the wooden counter in the foreground: an iron kettle, a cup of coffee, a small vase with a blossoming branch. A paper lantern hangs at top right, an indigo noren curtain at top left. Through the window: a narrow street of two-storey wooden shop fronts after rain, warm amber light glowing through paper shoji screens, hanging lanterns, a café opposite with a vermilion awning, a telegraph pole and sagging wires, wet street with long broken reflections. Moonlit olive-gold sky; umber walls; glowing gold windows. Flat areas of colour, crisp black key-block outlines, visible woodgrain and washi paper texture, limited palette. No people, no text, no signature, no border.
